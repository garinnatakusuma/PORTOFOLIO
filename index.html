About me

Hi, I'm Garin Kusuma. I've been designing footwear for the past four years, mostly because I'm just a curious guy who likes to see how we live in Indonesia. I have this habit of picking up random things — unique objects, weird stories, or odd experiences — and somehow connecting them all together. People might think it's a bit 'strange' or 'out there,' but I actually love the chaos in my head.

Right now, what you see here are my design narratives, but I'm working on making them a reality very soon. To support this, I also serve as a Design Drafter for automated manufacturing, with a focus on pallet design and computer stitching processes.

Smooth Criminal Jacket

Fashion apparel. A jacket that blends Javanese cultural heritage with the iconic style of the 'King of Pop,' Michael Jackson. This project explores premium lambskin leather and traditional Sorjan-patterned woven fabric (Tenun) to create an innovative and luxurious garment. Originally an academic project, it was selected for a fashion show at the PDIN (National Industrial Design Center) building.

Classic Street culture Leather tech

Includes story writing, moodboard, technical pattern making (leather & tenun), and full production workflow.
