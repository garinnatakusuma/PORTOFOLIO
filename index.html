About Me

Hi, I'm Garin Kusuma. I’ve been designing footwear for the past four years, mostly because I’m just a curious guy who likes to see how we live in Indonesia. I have this habit of picking up random things—unique objects, weird stories, or odd experiences—and somehow connecting them all together. People might think it’s a bit 'strange' or 'out there,' but I actually love the chaos in my head.

Right now, what you see here are my design narratives, but I’m working on making them a reality very soon. To support this, I also serve as a Design Drafter for automated manufacturing, with a focus on pallet design and computer stitching processes.

College Projects

Project College: SMOOTH CRIMINAL JACKET

Fashion Apparel. A jacket that seamlessly blends Javanese cultural heritage with the iconic style of the 'King of Pop,' Michael Jackson. This project explores the use of premium lambskin leather and traditional Sorjan-patterned woven fabric (Tenun) to create an innovative and luxurious garment. Originally developed as an academic project, this piece was selected to be showcased in a prestigious fashion show at the PDIN (National Industrial Design Center) building.

Classic Street Culture Leather Tech

*Details include: Story Writing, Moodboard, Technical Pattern Making (Leather & Tenun), and Full Production Workflow.

Project 3: DARK SYSTEM

Lifestyle Defiance & Social Connectivity. The 'Dark System' is a term that highlights the systemic oppression faced by marginalized groups. These shoes are a defiant statement against such injustices, serving as a visual manifesto for those who refuse to be silenced. Every pair is a declaration of our refusal to be silent in the face of oppression and a statement that we are all equal.
