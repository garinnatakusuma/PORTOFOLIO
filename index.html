01 / About the designer

Curiosity is where the concept begins.

Hi, I'm Garin Kusuma. I've been designing footwear for the past four years, mostly because I'm a curious person who likes to observe how we live in Indonesia. I often collect seemingly unrelated things — unique objects, unusual stories, and everyday experiences — then connect them into a design narrative.

What you see here is a collection of those narratives translated into footwear concepts. Alongside concept development, I also work as a Design Drafter for automated manufacturing, focusing on pallet design and computer stitching processes. That combination allows me to explore both the expressive side of design and the technical details required to make products.

ACADEMIC PROJECT / 2023

Smooth Criminal Jacket

A jacket concept that blends Javanese cultural heritage with the iconic visual language of Michael Jackson. The project explores premium lambskin leather and traditional Sorjan-patterned woven fabric (Tenun) to create a distinctive garment with a refined, luxurious character.

ClassicStreet cultureLeather technologyTenun

Documentation includes story writing, moodboard development, technical pattern making for leather and woven fabric, and the full production workflow. Selected for a fashion show at the PDIN (National Industrial Design Center) building.
